| Workflow | Description |
|---|---|
| `account_research` | Deep-dive research on a company: full account intelligence, recent buying signals, key contacts, and a synthesized brief. Start here when you are new to an account. |
| `account_brief` | A one-page brief you can read in two minutes before a call. Tight, current, and focused on what changed and what to say. |
| `account_plan` | Build a complete strategic account plan: overview, stakeholder map, SWOT, whitespace, competition, objectives, and a 90-day action plan. |
| `value_pyramid` | Build a value pyramid for an account: their company goals, business strategy, challenges and obstacles, and the value paths that connect your solution to their goals. |
| `meddic_qualification` | Qualify an opportunity against MEDDIC — metrics, economic buyer, decision criteria, decision process, identified pain, and champion — and expose the gaps that put the deal at risk. |
| `stakeholder_map` | Map the buying committee at an account: who holds power, who is likely to champion, who could block, and where the coverage gaps are. |
| `meeting_prep` | Prepare for a meeting with a specific person: their background and priorities, what changed at their company, conversation starters, and questions worth asking. |
| `signal_to_outreach` | Turn a specific buying signal into outreach that earns a reply — anchored to a real event, written for a real person, with a soft and specific ask. |
| `pipeline_signals` | Scan your accounts for recent buying signals and turn them into a prioritized action list. Use for a daily or weekly territory review. |
| `territory_review` | Step back and assess a whole book of accounts: where the potential is, which accounts are being under-worked, and where to spend time next quarter. |
| `earnings_deep_dive` | Mine a public company's earnings calls and filings for strategy, priorities, and pressure in management's own words — the most credible material for executive conversations. |
| `hiring_signals_analysis` | Read a company's job postings as strategy: what they are building, which teams are funded, what tools they use, and what that means for timing. |
